03 / METHODOLOGY
Methodology

1. Landmark detection

FACET uses MediaPipe Face Landmarker to identify facial landmarks directly from the uploaded photographs.

2. Individual geometry

Measurements are derived from the individual's own landmark geometry. FACET does not assume that every person's face has the same physical width or that every photograph was taken from the same distance.

3. Ratios and angles

Whenever possible, distances are expressed relative to another landmark distance on the same face and angles are calculated directly from landmark coordinates. This makes the calculation independent of image size.

4. Reference ranges

Reference ranges are displayed separately from the measured value. The comparison number represents mathematical distance from the reference interval and is not an attractiveness score.

5. Unavailable measurements

A measurement is marked unavailable when the necessary anatomical information cannot be obtained reliably from an ordinary frontal or profile photograph. FACET does not invent missing measurements.
